Stirling Engine — Analysis, CAD & Fabrication
ME Tools Lab · May 2025 · Florida State University
SolidWorksGD&TThermodynamicsManual MachiningAssembly Modeling

Project Overview

A complete design-to-build exercise spanning thermodynamic analysis, full SolidWorks CAD modeling, toleranced drawing production, and hands-on fabrication. The finished engine ran under applied heat, validating both the computational model and the dimensional stack-up.

Thermodynamic Analysis

  • Analyzed ideal and real Stirling thermodynamic cycles, evaluating a gamma engine at 9.8% thermal efficiency
  • Modeled heat transfer, frictional losses, thermal leakage, and phase lag to explain deviation from ideal performance

CAD Design & Fabrication

  • Modeled all major subsystems in SolidWorks: piston/cylinder assembly, flywheel, displacer, and frame
  • Machined all components from raw stock on manual mill and lathe

Who I Am

Designer. Innovator. Contributor.

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at the FAMU-FSU College of Engineering driven by a passion for designing products that make a real difference. I thrive on bringing ideas from initial concept to working prototype by pairing 3D CAD modeling with precision machining and hands-on fabrication.

My path at Florida State University began in the Engineering Living Learning Community, where team challenges in the First-Year Engineering Lab (FYEL) first hooked me on collaborative design. As my studies advanced through courses like Materials Science, Mechatronics, and the Mechanical Engineering Tools Lab, my interests expanded into nanotechnology, adaptive control, and advanced CAD. Outside the classroom, I earned equipment certifications through the campus Makerspace and designed custom components using Formlabs SLA printers at the FSU Innovation Hub.

Fascinated by the intersection of biotechnology, mechatronics, and product design, I joined Generational Relief in Prosthetics (GRiP) to build custom assistive devices for individuals with limb differences. A major highlight was collaborating with my project team to design and fabricate a fully functional, elbow-actuated prosthetic arm for a 7-year-old girl with Type II Radial Aplasia.

I am actively seeking Summer 2027 internships in Product Design, Medical Technology, and Industrial Automation, fields where I can leverage my full design-to-build skillset to solve impactful, real-world problems.
